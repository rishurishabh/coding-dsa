Queue: in at the back, out at the front

Enqueue adds at the back, dequeue removes from the front — two different ends, the mirror image of Stack's single end. Code lives in data-structures/02-queue/*.js.

01-array-queue.js

Array-backed, circular buffer

head/tail indices wrap instead of the array physically shifting on dequeue.

02-linked-list-queue.js

Linked list, head + tail pointers

Without a tail pointer, append would need to walk the whole list first — O(n), not O(1).

03-queue-using-two-stacks.js

FIFO from two LIFO stacks

out refills from in only when empty — each element moves at most twice, ever.

04-sliding-window-maximum.js

Monotonic deque

Kept in decreasing order — each index pushed once, popped at most once — O(n) total.

05-design-circular-queue.js

Fixed-capacity circular queue

head === tail is ambiguous on its own (empty or full?) — an explicit count resolves it.
