Stack: everything happens at one end

Push adds on top, pop removes from the top — no other position is ever touched directly. That restriction is what makes both operations O(1). Code lives in data-structures/01-stack/*.js.

O(1) minimum tracking

Every push records "the min so far, including this value" — no scan needed to answer getMin().
