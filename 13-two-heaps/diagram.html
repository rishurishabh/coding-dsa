Two Heaps: the boundary IS the answer

A max-heap holds everything below the median, a min-heap holds everything above it. Keep them the same size (±1) and the median is always sitting at one of their tops. Code lives in 13-two-heaps/*.js.

Two balanced heaps

Balanced heaps + lazy deletion

Gate heap + pick heap

One heap orders what's locked, the other orders what's currently open — capital only grows, never shrinks the options.
