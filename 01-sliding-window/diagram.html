Sliding Window: the mechanism, and its nine shapes

What changes is what the window remembers

01-fixed-size.js

Fixed-size window

k is fixed at 3. Each slide drops one cell (−) and adds one (+); the window never resizes.

02-variable-size.js

Variable-size window

Grow right until the sum clears the target, then shrink left as long as it still holds — smallest window wins (amber).

03-at-most-k.js

"At most K" → "exactly K"

atMost(K) counts a wide bracket; atMost(K−1) counts a narrower one nested inside it. The gap between them (amber) is exactly K.

04-frequency-map.js

Window + frequency counter

Grow the window until the "need" counter hits zero for every character, then shrink left while it still can.

05-monotonic-deque.js

Monotonic deque

A bigger newcomer pops every smaller value behind it before joining — the front is always the window's current max.

06-two-pointer-last-seen.js

Last-seen-index jump

Instead of stepping left forward one cell at a time, jump it straight to one past the duplicate's last-seen index — O(1).

07-at-most-k-changes.js

"At most K violations" window

The window stays valid while it holds at most K "wrong" cells (dashed rose) — grow right, shrink left only past K.

08-prefix-sum-deque.js

Prefix sum + monotonic deque

P[j] closes out the earliest prefix P[i] with P[j]−P[i] ≥ k. The deque keeps only prefixes small enough to still matter — survives negatives.

09-bitmask-window.js

Bitmask window

mask = OR of the window's bits. A newcomer that shares a bit forces XOR-evictions from the left until the conflict clears.
