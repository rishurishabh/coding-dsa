Binary search: one comparison, half the candidates gone

Every variant halves the search space on every step. What changes is what gets compared at the midpoint — a target, a structure, a feasibility check, or just a neighbor. Code lives in 16-modified-binary-search/*.js.

Keep narrowing past a match

06-binary-search-on-answer.js

07-find-peak-element.js

No target, no global sort — a peak is guaranteed to exist in whichever direction the slope climbs.
