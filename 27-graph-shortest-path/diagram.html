Five variants

Same question, five different constraints on weights or hops

01-dijkstra.js

Greedy heap expansion

Only safe when every edge weight is non-negative — the baseline every other variant bends.

02-bellman-ford.js

Round-based edge relaxation

Works with negative weights — trades Dijkstra's O(E log V) for O(V·E), no heap needed.

03-01-bfs.js

Deque instead of a heap

Dijkstra's ordering guarantee, kept with O(1) deque pushes since weights are only 0 or 1.

04-floyd-warshall.js

DP over intermediate vertices

Not single-source at all — a completely different shape of answer, DP not greedy at all.

05-cheapest-flights-within-k-stops.js

Bounded relaxation rounds

Round i already means "at most i edges" — the hop limit falls out of Bellman-Ford for free.
