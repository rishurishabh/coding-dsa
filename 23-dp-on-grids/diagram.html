Five variants

Same grid, until the neighbor count or fill direction breaks

01-unique-paths.js

Sum of two neighbors

First row and column each have exactly one way in — the base case for the rest.

02-unique-paths-with-obstacles.js

Same recurrence, one guard

No new recurrence — a blocked cell just can't contribute any paths at all.

03-minimum-path-sum.js

Min of two neighbors

Variant 1's `+` swapped for `min` — same two-neighbor shape, different operator.

04-maximal-square.js

Min of THREE neighbors

A square is only as strong as its weakest supporting side — needs all three.

05-dungeon-game.js

Same shape, filled backward

What a cell needs depends on cells AHEAD of it — the fill direction has to flip.
