Monotonic Stack: popping IS the answer

Keep the stack sorted by popping anything the new element beats. Every variant reads those pops differently — as a lookup, a pair of lookups, or a greedy build. Code lives in 07-monotonic-stack/*.js.

Six variants

01-next-greater-element.js

Decreasing stack, resolve on pop

The popped bar's answer wasn't knowable until this later, bigger bar showed up.

02-previous-greater-element.js

Decreasing stack, resolve immediately

Whatever survives on top of the stack after popping IS the current bar's answer, immediately.

03-circular-next-greater.js

Variant 1, walked around twice

i % n replays the array a second time so wraparound candidates get a chance to resolve earlier bars.

04-largest-rectangle-histogram.js

Increasing stack, both directions at once

A bar's max rectangle spans from its nearest-smaller-left to its nearest-smaller-right — both found in one pass.

05-trapping-rain-water-stack.js

Decreasing stack, resolve a region

Popping the short floor bar, bounded by the new bar and whatever's exposed behind it, resolves one water layer.

06-remove-k-digits.js

Greedy stack construction

A bigger digit gets popped the moment a smaller one follows it — each pop spends one unit of the removal budget.
